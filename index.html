View the complete model formulas +
Annual hours = employees × hours saved per week × working weeks × adoption rate.
Annual capacity value = annual hours × hourly cost.
Annual net value = annual capacity value − annual recurring cost.
First-year net value = annual net value − one-time cost.
First-year ROI = first-year net value ÷ one-time cost. Simple payback = one-time cost ÷ positive annual net value × 12 months.
